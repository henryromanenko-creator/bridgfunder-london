Asset Finance

Logistics Fleet Expansion

The Challenge: A thriving SME logistics firm won a major new contract but needed to rapidly expand their fleet without draining their operational cash reserves.

The Execution: Instead of diluting their equity or tying up working capital, we structured a highly tax-efficient hire-purchase facility for 5 new commercial vehicles, allowing them to service the contract immediately.
